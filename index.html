About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Engineering at USC, working in the FPGA/Parallel Computing Lab, advised by Prof. Viktor K. Prasanna and Prof. Raj Kannan.

My research interests include machine learning for systems, graph machine learning, agentic and physical AI, subquadratic neural network architectures, and heterogeneous computing.
